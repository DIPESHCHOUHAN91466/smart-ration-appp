# Citizen guide

For ration-card holders. You need a mobile phone or computer with a browser; the pages work on a phone.

## Without an account

- **Home page** — what the service does, in your language.
- **Public Help** — topics such as documents, booking and QR codes, with a search box.
- **Ration Mitra** (chat button) — ask in English, Hindi or Marathi. It answers from reviewed information
  and links to the right page. Never type your Aadhaar number, an OTP or a password into it — it will refuse
  them for your protection. When you are signed in and ask about *your* booking, it shows only your own.

## Register and sign in

**Register** with your name, e-mail, mobile number and a password (at least 8 characters). Your ration
card, family and shop are linked to the account (in this demo they are created for you, marked synthetic).

## Book a collection slot — Book Ration

1. **Shop** — choose your fair-price shop.
2. **Slot** — pick a date and a time. Each slot is 5 minutes and takes a limited number of people; full
   slots are not offered.
3. **Items** — choose from the items your card is entitled to this month (the quantities follow your scheme
   and family size).
4. **Confirm** — check the shop, time and items, then confirm.

You receive a **token** with a number (for example `SR-2026-000132`) and a **QR code**.

## At the shop — My Token

Open the token and show the QR code on your phone (or the printed reference under it). The shop scans it;
the QR contains no personal data — only a signed reference that the server checks. A QR is valid for its
booking only; a used, cancelled or expired QR is refused. If scanning fails, the shop can verify you with a
one-time code (OTP) sent to your registered mobile number.

You can **cancel** a booking from the same page while it has not been collected; the slot is freed for
someone else.

## Other pages

- **Booking History** — past and upcoming bookings with their status.
- **My Verification** — whether your mobile number, Aadhaar reference (masked) and ration-card passbook are
  verified.
- **Notifications** — booking confirmations and updates; times are shown in your local time and language.
- **Settings** — language, your name and mobile number.

If something looks wrong (for example an item is missing from your entitlement), ask at your shop or your
district office; the app shows the rules but does not change them.
