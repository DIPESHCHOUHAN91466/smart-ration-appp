## Who the app is for

- **Citizens (ration card holders):** book a time to collect ration, show a QR token at the shop, and
  see their ration card, family members, eligibility and collection history.
- **Ration shop owners:** check a customer's token and hand over ration.
- **Government officials:** see shops, stock and alerts.

## What information the app uses

The app shows information that is held by the Smart Ration service. It does not create this
information itself.

| Information | Why it is used |
| --- | --- |
| Your name, mobile number, email address and role | To sign you in and show your account |
| Ration card, family members, scheme and eligibility | To show what you are entitled to |
| Bookings, tokens and collection history | To book a time and collect your ration |
| Notifications (e.g. "Ration collected") | To tell you about your bookings and collections |
| Questions you ask the help assistant | To answer them (see "Help assistant" below) |

**Aadhaar:** the app never asks for your Aadhaar number. It only shows whether Aadhaar is verified,
and a shop owner never sees your Aadhaar number. Mobile numbers are shown to shop owners in masked
form (for example `******0001`).

## Permissions

- **Internet:** to talk to the Smart Ration service.
- **Camera (shop owners only):** to read the customer's QR code. The picture stays on the phone;
  only the text inside the QR code is sent to the service to be checked. No photos are saved.
- **Microphone:** only when you tap the microphone button in the help chat, and only after you allow
  it. Your phone's speech service (for example Google's) turns your voice into text and may use the
  internet to do so, under that service's own privacy policy. The app receives only the text. It
  does not record or keep any audio.

The app does **not** use your location, contacts, photos, files or call log.

## What is kept on your phone

- Your sign-in session, so you stay signed in.
- A copy of your upcoming ration tokens and their QR codes, so you can show them at the shop
  without internet.
- Your chosen language.

The session and the saved tokens are kept in **encrypted storage** that other apps cannot read.
They are **deleted when you sign out** (or when your session ends), and they are **not included in
phone or cloud backups**. Help-chat conversations are not saved on the phone; they disappear when
you close the chat.

## Help assistant ("Ration Mitra")

Questions you type or speak are sent to the Smart Ration service and answered from its own built-in
help pages; they are not sent to any outside AI company. The service does not save the text of your
questions, in its logs or anywhere else. Please do not type Aadhaar numbers, OTPs or
passwords in the chat; the assistant will refuse them. If you are signed in as a citizen and ask
about your own bookings, the answer uses your account.

## Sharing

We do not sell your information, and the app contains **no advertising and no tracking or analytics
tools**. Your information is shared only as needed to provide the ration service: with your ration
shop (to serve you) and with government officials who oversee the public distribution system,
[AND ANY OTHER PARTIES REQUIRED BY LAW OR BY THE SCHEME — OPERATOR TO CONFIRM].

## Security

The release app talks to the service only over encrypted connections (https). QR codes are signed by
the service so a copied or changed code is rejected at the shop. Sign-in sessions expire and can be
ended by signing out.

## Your choices and rights

- You can sign out at any time; this removes your data from the phone.
- You can turn off the microphone permission in your phone's settings.
- To correct your ration card or family details, contact your ration office.

## Children

The app is meant for adult ration card holders, shop owners and officials. Family members' details,
including children's, are shown only to the card holder and to the people who serve them, as part of
the ration record.

## Test versions

Test versions of the app may use **synthetic (made-up) demo data**. They are clearly marked "Demo
data: this is not a real ration card."
